# Two-Sentence Horror

A two-sentence horror story entry for the make.fun bounty "Two-Sentence Horror". The program `main.py` writes one story to `/output/entry.txt` — every night a phone buzzes with a photo of its owner asleep, taken from the foot of the bed, until the newest photo shows the owner awake, smiling, holding the phone.

> Every night at 3am my phone buzzes with a photo of me asleep, taken from the foot of my bed. This morning the newest one showed me awake, smiling, holding the phone.

Run it with `python3 main.py`. It asserts the entry is 1–400 characters, exactly two sentences (exactly two periods, no other sentence-ending punctuation), with no leading or trailing whitespace, then writes it with no trailing newline.
